There are four weapons in Cell Block A.
Here are some groovy renders of em.
 


Cool Renders:
 
 


Hands: Curl em up into fists and use 'em to knock your opponent to a horrible death below, or just beat em down.

 
 
 

Rebar: Basically an extension of your fists, lay the smack down from afar.


Fire Extinguisher: Spray your opponent to stun 'em and then beat 'em down... and over the edge!

 
 
 


Shotgun: Guards use 'em to keep you in line. You use 'em to show your enemy who's boss.
