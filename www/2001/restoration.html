The 2001 website

These are modern preservation notes, added October 15, 2026. The four historical pages are restored from their original CuteHTML source, preserving their wording, blue background, gray text, cyan links, image-button navigation, table layout, and original copyright notice.

Source pages

  • Main: January 29, 2001.
  • Screens: May 3, 2001.
  • Download: May 3, 2001.
  • Links: May 3, 2001.

Surviving image-button and illustration captures span 2001–2002. The second screenshot and its thumbnail survive at the earlier site paths, so those 2000 copies supply the missing pics/shot2a.gif and pics/shot2b.gif. This is a composite restoration; identical artwork at every date cannot be established.

Missing assets and changes

  • The three decorative header strips (topbar-top.jpg, topbar-middle.jpg, topbar-bottom.jpg) were not recovered. Their image references are removed, leaving the original charcoal table color and row dimensions.
  • The selected Download and Links buttons were not recovered. Their surviving unselected images are reused, with the current page still unlinked. Main and Screens retain their recovered selected states.
  • The original “OCR A Extended” and “Terminal” font requests are retained, with monospace fallbacks for systems without those fonts. No font files are bundled; typography will vary.
  • The remote Honesty.com visitor counter is omitted. Its original “Since: 01/23/01” caption remains.
  • All installer mirror links point to the shared local original 1.3 installer. The original mirror labels remain, although they no longer select different servers.
  • The map-pack link points to the recovered maps01.zip, from the June 3, 2001 capture. The ZIP is unmodified; it has not been installed into the reconstructed game.
  • Image alternative text, document language, restoration notes, and a link to the version selector are added.

The original layouts are intentionally fixed-width. In particular, the Screens page's two-column gallery expands beyond the 611-pixel header, as specified by its original tables.

Preservation evidence

Original HTML lives in ../_archive/original/2001/. Shared graphics and download provenance are recorded in the manifest. The README explains offline rebuilding and verification.
